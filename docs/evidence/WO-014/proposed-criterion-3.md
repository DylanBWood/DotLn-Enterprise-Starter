# Criterion 3 amendment — approved by the operator

Both independent checkers admitted Team Topologies' two typed rows. The other
635 mapped rows remain available as untyped candidates or background concepts;
their conceptual usefulness is separate from transform compatibility. The
bounded metaphor pass does not seek a third transform to fill the quota.

Proposed replacement for criterion 3:

> `npm run catalog:report` exits 0 and prints, for each of the six works, a
> primary scene type and up to three tools whose transform kind is a member of
> `TransformKind`. It records the shortfall from three explicitly. A spec asserts
> the valid typed examples and the correctly computed shortfall over
> `catalog-summary.json`. Each example retains its own applicable scene types;
> the work's primary scene is a recommendation, not a constraint on every example.

This retains the closed transform contract and exposes the shortfall. The
concept-mapping method and optional practical applications carry the operator's
broader intent, including metaphors and diagnostic uses of anti-patterns.

The order's known-issues clause explicitly says a short work is recorded and
criterion 3 is amended on the operator's word. The operator explicitly approved this amendment in the session; the order now
records it. Approval does not itself establish a passing criterion judgment.
