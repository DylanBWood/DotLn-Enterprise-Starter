## Proposed enrichment program

During execution the operator asked how this depth will extend to the entire
catalog. The executor's proposed next phase is a content-enrichment program,
with a full inventory and explicit backlog, rather than treating the single
Arrive example as the finished vision. This sequence is planning input; it
does not schedule thousands of studies into WO-019:

1. Define separate, versioned concept-study records for intuition, worked
   examples, mechanisms, assumptions, limits, relationships, references,
   implementations, pseudocode and visuals. Each explanation records its
   source and review state, and survives regeneration of the mined data.
2. Pilot roughly 12–20 varied concepts across the six works. Include different
   teaching problems, not only movement algorithms. Measure effort and review
   quality before forecasting the full catalog.
3. Extract reusable visual families from the pilot: motion, timelines, stream
   marbles, graph flows, state transitions and relationship maps. Select media
   per explanation; do not force RxJS or a particular renderer onto every idea.
4. Enrich bounded batches through research, authorship, independent factual
   review, executable checks where applicable, and rendered review. Validate
   that cited sources support the claims, not only that links respond.
5. Track missing, draft and checked coverage explicitly. Shared studies can
   serve related or repeated entries while preserving source-specific meaning.
   The operator's comparisons establish reusable patterns; new teaching forms
   and meaningful exceptions return for further comparison.

The next planning pass should assign ownership of both the authoring system and
