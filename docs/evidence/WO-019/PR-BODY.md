Catalog readers can choose one of six works, browse its contents, read complete definitions, follow related concepts, search and return to their ten recent readings after a reload. The data join preserves the mapping run's content and provenance, and the required steering and formation tools carry typed scene contracts.

Arrive adds a separately authored study with controlled movement and velocity views, a worked calculation, ordinary and RxJS pseudocode, limits and public references. Closed explanations load on first expansion. The workspace scripts now state their purpose and distinguish gate checks from occasional helpers.

Validation covers workspace lint, unit tests, formatting, builds, browser and visual checks. Independent production journeys passed at 360, 768 and 1440 pixels, including expanded-study accessibility and malformed saved state.
