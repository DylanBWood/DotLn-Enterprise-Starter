# Add the soldier scene with flock, arrive and formation

## Summary

The root now opens the soldier scene. Twenty units idle on a dark field that fills the page below the shell's header, each labelled with a lifecycle role and coloured by it, with a second label set for the five UIFA roles behind a toggle. A rail on the right lists the sixteen AI for Games tools the engine runs, grouped by what they do (move, group, avoid, form), each name in its work's colour, each definition read from the catalog. Units are selected the way a real-time strategy game does it: a marquee, a click, a shift-click, a double-click for a unit's squad, Escape to clear, or Tab and Space through the text layer under the field, which carries the same state as text. A chosen tool shows its definition in a reading measure, its five faces as a sentence, its parameters as sliders with the catalog's defaults, and the first frame's pull as faint arrows on the selected units; Apply puts it on them and starts the run, and a slider moved afterwards reaches the same units at once, each change recorded as a step. A click on the field sets the objective and a right-click sets it and gives the selected units Arrive, as a move order does. The two-level formation sorts units into wedge, line or column slots, the hardest to place first, from a cost table shown in the inspector with a live count of who has reached their slot. An instrument strip shows cohesion radius, collisions, heading variance, time to objective and stalled units as large figures with sparklines of the last ten seconds, reading the selected units or everyone. Compare rewinds to the last equip and replays the branch before it as a ghost under the branch after it, two series per metric with the first divergence frame marked; a kept trial, the steps and a note save as a composition in the browser, can be exported and imported as JSON, and come back after a reload.

The movement engine is pure TypeScript in its own shared library behind a renderer seam with a canvas painter, a text painter and a null painter, so a later painter changes neither the engine nor the feature, and the feature's specs run under jsdom on the null one. A run is a seed and a list of steps and replays frame for frame; a committed known outcome pins the hashes and metric series, and the engine keeps native trigonometry out so the hashes reproduce on every machine. The engine reads a catalog tool through its parameter names, so a typed row with known names runs without code of its own, and a step whose kind it does not interpret is refused with a reason naming the kind and leaves the run as if the step had never been made. The rules the scene adds beyond the catalog are recorded in the scene architecture document: velocity matching cruises until an objective drives it, forces fade at their edges rather than switching, a group has arrived when its centre is inside the radius and every part of it is there or when it has stayed slow round the objective for half a second, and the stopwatch restarts only when a unit newly heads for the objective.

Around the scene: a field skin as a token layer under `[data-skin='field']` that reaches the shell through tokens alone; `dotln-button` gains `size` and `tone`; the shared persistence feature gains `writeOn`, `unreadable` and `unwritable`, so the scene writes only on Save and reports a stored value it cannot read without rewriting it; the catalog reading's soldier-scene line becomes a link that arrives with the tool chosen; the not-found page links home to the scene; the arriving page and the board's root redirect are gone, and the board keeps `/board`. No runtime dependency was added. The scene is its own lazy chunk of 102.50 kB raw; the initial bundle is 398.22 kB raw, 4.59 kB over the previous release, inside the budgets.

## Verification

The independent verification performed the session the order describes against a fresh production build at 360, 768 and 1440 pixels, with real pointer and keyboard actions and no injected state: the root landing on the scene, a marquee round twelve units, Flocking read and applied, an objective and Arrive, eight more units and the wedge, Compare and a kept trial, Save, reload and the composition found, and the catalog's link arriving with Flocking chosen. Its 24 captures and its own reading of the instruments are in the order's evidence: cohesion radius from 37.8 for the twelve to about 15 after Flocking and 2.9 after Arrive with collisions at zero throughout and time to objective 5.0 s; the wedge taking the eight from 32.2 to 8.1 with all eight in their slots. Independent probes compared 48 varied seeds and tool sequences over 600 frames live and in replay with every hash agreeing, saw collisions peak at 50 with separation removed and stay at zero with the defaults, matched the hand-computed slot table and the committed outcome, and found a saved composition writing only its own key with empty cookies and no request leaving the browser. Axe reported no violation at the three widths, and nothing overflowed. Every criterion was met; one Low follow-up was recorded, a text-layer checkbox left checked when a toggle and Escape land before one render.

The declared review gate (install, lint, test, format, build, e2e and visual) passed at the verified tree with no tracked-byte mutation after the preflight, and passed again at the reviewed tree after the review's repair below.
